        THE LITTLE RULES

        Gather up to five treats, then hold Space for 0.85 seconds to eat them all and earn points. Letting go early cancels the bite. Uneaten treats score nothing when time runs out. Highest score wins; equal scores share the win.

        Q gives a short dash: 12 energy, four seconds between dashes. A forward bump spills at most one uneaten treat and gives the bumped hamster two seconds of protection. Ordinary nudges never spill treats. Your banked score is always safe.

        Use Shift to walk and restore energy. E enters or leaves the wheel near its open front. Only one hamster fits in the wheel; running earns 10 points every five seconds, up to three times per round. All players share the same three-minute clock.

        Face the water spout and hold Space for 1.2 seconds for 2 points and 8 energy, with a five-second cooldown. Walk into the pastel tubes: W moves, S turns around, A / D chooses the fork. Eating and exploring work just like HAMSTER. Tubes and the wheel are sheltered from bumps.

        Six golden treats appear every 30 seconds, rotating between the bedding, wooden loft and lookout deck. Pick them up within ten seconds, then eat them for 50 points each. The host can pause for everyone; a hidden host tab pauses the picnic. If a guest disconnects, everyone returns to the lobby. If the host leaves, the room ends.
